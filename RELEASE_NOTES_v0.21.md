# F6 Club Manager v0.21

## New

- Offer a player one or several teams from either their Decision tab or the Email Centre.
- Select a playing position and expected squad role for every team option.
- Available squad roles: Starting six, Frequent player, Rotational player, Development / improvement role, Training squad and Role to be discussed.
- Multi-team emails list every option with its team, position, role and available team details.
- Offer wording asks for a response within 72 hours of the trial session and asks players to contact the club if they need more time.
- Accepted players can be assigned to the exact option they chose; only that team becomes their confirmed squad.

## Improved

- Email pre-send checks validate every option independently.
- Team-capacity warnings cover every offered team and position.
- Email queue labels, searching and CSV export include all offered teams.
- Team Planner offer counts understand multi-team options.
- Editing an already-sent offer returns it to draft review so outdated wording cannot be recorded accidentally.

## Compatibility

- Existing single-team Firebase offers are converted automatically when loaded.
- No Firebase database migration, rules update or additional GitHub secret is required.
